Common questions.

Tell us on GitHub ↗

Do I need cloud sync to use ShellPort?

No. Add hosts and use the terminal without configuring sync. WebDAV is optional: connect your own service and choose what to sync when you need it.

Can I use a local terminal too?

Yes. Open a local terminal from the hosts page without creating an SSH host. Remote servers still connect over SSH.

Are private keys included in sync or backups?

No. Locally saved SSH private keys are excluded from exported backups and cloud sync. Add them again on a new device. Login passwords follow your selected scope. Backups contain configuration such as host addresses, so keep them safe.

Where can I find older versions and release notes?

Find past versions, release notes, and packages in Releases. You can also share issues and ideas on GitHub.

What should I include in an issue report?

Include your operating system, processor architecture, ShellPort version, reproduction steps, and expected and actual results. Screenshots or sanitized logs help. Do not upload passwords, private keys, databases, local encryption key files, or unsanitized logs.
